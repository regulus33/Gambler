# Gambler privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Gambler does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The app does not send anything over the internet. The only exception is **⋯ → Report a bug**, which opens a GitHub page in your browser; nothing is sent unless you submit the form there yourself.
- Presets and settings are saved only on your device.
- If iOS ever shows a microphone or Bluetooth permission prompt, it is only for audio or MIDI connections on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/regulus33/Gambler/issues
