Screenshots

These are iPad 13" landscape (2752 × 2064), the size App Store Connect asks for; it scales them down for smaller iPads. The app screens inside are real iPad mini landscape captures (source/mini-scale.png, source/mini-melody.png), whose proportions suit the UI best. To change them, retake those captures and run ./scripts/render-appstore-screenshots.sh. The app is iPad-only, so no iPhone set is needed.

Privacy & App Review

App Privacy questionnaire
Answer “No, we do not collect data from this app.” The app makes no network calls, has no analytics, accounts or ads (checked in source/), so the label reads “Data Not Collected”.
Age rating questionnaire
